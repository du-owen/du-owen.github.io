Austrian-born Chinese DJ and founder of BAZA.8ZA, promoting underground global sounds and culture. Making people dance anywhere from Berlin to Zurich and now Beijing. du du blends electronic music with lyrical samples from all across the globe, from Latin America to East Asia and further. His sets trace the diaspora of rhythm: Funk carioca percussion meeting modular synth, East Asian pop fragments dissolving into UK bass weight. Every transition is a conversation between places he’s lived and places he’s listened from afar.

Since landing in Beijing, dudu has been collabing with key artists from the Beijing underground scene and most recently rising Chinese DJ Purp and LA-based popstar CAM GIRL, attracting thousands to Beijing's underground scene. By day, he’s a Master’s student at Tsinghua and ETH Zurich, researching AI.
