BAZA.8ZA is a mobile ecosystem for global club culture. Born in Zurich in 2025 and transplanted to Beijing, BAZA operates as a platform for cross-border collaborations, unearthing raw talent from a genre-fluid scene that serves a stepping stone into non-mainstream electronic music.

Since landing in Beijing, BAZA has been collabing with key artists from the Beijing underground scene and most recently rising Chinese DJ Purp and LA-based popstar CAM GIRL, attracting thousands to Beijing's underground scene.
